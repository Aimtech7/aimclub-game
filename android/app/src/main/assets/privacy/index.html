Privacy & local control

Effective 18 October 2026. Aimfun Club is for adults aged 18 and older.

On your device

Player names, local game sessions, prompt history, scores, theme preferences and submitted drawing chains are stored in browser storage. This data stays on that browser unless you export it. Secret-role sessions contain their hidden information locally so they can resume. Age confirmation is stored for the browser session; no date of birth is collected.

Online rooms

Creating or joining a room sends your nickname and adult confirmation. The server stores room membership, scores, game state, secret words and submitted answers to operate the room. Clients receive a restricted view; group totals and final game results appear when a round ends. Rooms expire after 24 hours, and expired records are removed on later room creation. Hashed network identifiers and minute-based counters limit abuse; counters expire after two minutes and are removed during room cleanup.

Chat, calls and spectators

Room chat stores the latest 80 messages until the room is closed or expires. The host can clear chat. Emoji reactions appear briefly. Call signaling is delivered only to its intended room member and is retained for up to two minutes before later room activity removes it. Voice/video streams travel between call participants and are not recorded by Aimfun. Joining a call requests microphone and, optionally, camera permission. Calls use Google’s public STUN service by default; peers and connection providers may receive network addresses. An operator-configured TURN relay may carry encrypted media. Leaving the call stops local camera/microphone tracks. Spectators see shared prompts, results, scores and room chat, but do not receive secret words or private answers. Hosts control spectator admission.

Profiles, packs and reminders

Nickname profiles, observed online rounds, achievements, favourite games, custom packs and visited scheduled nights are saved on this browser. They do not sync across devices and are not verified rankings. Loading a custom pack into a room uploads its prompts to the room server. Exported pack files and calendar invitations contain the information you chose to share. Calendar reminders run through your chosen calendar app. Optional browser reminders are checked while My Club remains open; Aimfun does not send background push notifications.

Reconnecting

A private reconnect token is stored on your device. Treat it like a password. Do not share browser backups or screenshots containing it. Room codes let people request access while the lobby is open. Hosts can lock joining and remove players from the lobby.

Backups and deletion

Local backups may contain names and saved game information. Keep them private. Clear this site's browser storage to remove local progress and reconnect tokens. Hosts can close a room to delete its game record immediately. Otherwise, online room data follows the expiration above. No advertising or third-party analytics is included by Aimfun Club. Hosting and network providers process requests needed to deliver the app.
