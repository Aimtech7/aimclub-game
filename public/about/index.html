Aimfun Club · 18+

One place for adults to connect through party games, puzzles and shared stories. Play solo, pass one device around, or use an online room for supported games.

Your online club

Create a room and share its link to play from different places. Supported online modes are Friends Truth or Dare, Spicy Couples, Quick Pick, Have You Ever, Undercover and your custom question packs. Chat, react, watch as a spectator or join a beta voice/video call. My Club holds local profiles, packs, scheduled nights and installation tools. Other games remain available on a shared device.

Choose your evening

Friends Truth or Dare offers 1,150 prompts. Spicy Couples offers 1,000 flirty, non-graphic prompts for consenting adults. Endless shuffle recycles only after a full deck has been played. It does not generate infinitely many unique prompts.

Your comfort comes first

Anyone can pass. Never pressure someone to reveal private information or accept physical contact. Romantic dares include solo alternatives. All players must be 18 or older.

Support

Questions or bug reports: aimnexventures@gmail.com. Include the game name, device/browser and what happened. Do not include private answers, secret room tokens or intimate personal information.

Android preview project

The Android project includes the offline game files and opens online rooms through the hosted site. It is source code for local building and testing, not a tested APK or a Play Store release.

Play fairly

Speaking, acting and secret-mission games rely on honest group judgement. Shared-device hidden-role games store their session locally; browser developer tools can expose local secrets. Online Undercover delivers only your own secret word to your device.
