Installation instructions

Android / Chrome: browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen. iPhone / iPad: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Desktop: use your browser’s install icon or menu. Wait for “Offline ready” before disconnecting. Microphone/camera and notification support depend on your browser.
